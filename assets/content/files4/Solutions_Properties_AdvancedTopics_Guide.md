## SECTION 2: THERMODYNAMIC PROPERTIES OF SOLUTIONS

### Table 2.1: Ideal Solution Thermodynamics

| Thermodynamic Quantity | Ideal Solution Expression | Physical Interpretation | Deviation from Ideality | Real Solution Behavior | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Enthalpy of Mixing** | ΔH_mix = 0 (ideal; no heat change) | No energy change upon mixing; intermolecular forces between A-A, B-B ≈ forces between A-B | Real: ΔH_mix ≠ 0; negative (exothermic, attractive A-B) or positive (endothermic, repulsive A-B) | Negative dev.: solution heats (HCl + water); Positive dev.: solution cools (dissolution some salts, sulfates) | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Entropy of Mixing** | ΔS_mix = −R Σ χ_i ln χ_i (Boltzmann/statistical mechanics) | Disorder increases from pure components to solution; probability-driven; configurational entropy | Same formula applies to real solutions (entropy always positive for mixing); differs from enthalpy | ΔS_mix unchanged between ideal/real; entropy always drives mixing (positive) at any T | 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Gibbs Free Energy of Mixing** | ΔG_mix = ΔH_mix − T ΔS_mix ≈ −T ΔS_mix (ideal; since ΔH_mix ≈ 0) | Mixing spontaneous at all T & composition (ideal solutions homogenize completely) | Real: ΔG_mix = ΔH_mix − T ΔS_mix; if ΔH_mix very positive, may be ΔG_mix > 0 (immiscible) | Negative dev. (attractive): ΔG_mix more negative (faster mixing); Positive dev.: ΔG_mix less negative (phase separation possible) | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Positive Deviation (Repulsive A-B)** | ΔH_mix > 0 (endothermic, requires heat) | ΔG_mix less negative than ideal (weaker spontaneity); possible phase separation if large | CS₂ + acetone (polarity mismatch), benzene + alcohol (disrupts H-bonding network), immiscible pairs | P_total > P_ideal (VP higher than Raoult's prediction); easier evaporation | Yes: maximum-boiling azeotrope (e.g., CS₂ + acetone, 0.02 mole fraction CS₂, ~40°C) | γ > 1 (activity > concentration; molecules prefer like neighbors) | 2022, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |

## SECTION 4: IDEAL VS. NON-IDEAL SOLUTIONS

### Table 4.1: Ideal Solution Properties vs. Real Solutions

| Property | Ideal Solution Definition | Real Solutions (Deviations) | Thermodynamic Consequence | Measurement | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ideal Solution** | Raoult's law holds exactly; intermolecular forces between unlike molecules (solute-solvent) similar to like molecules (solute-solute, solvent-solvent) | Real solutions: intermolecular forces differ; attractions or repulsions lead to deviations | ΔH_mix ≈ 0 (approximately); ΔS_mix = −R Σ χ_i ln χ_i (entropy of mixing, ideal form); ΔG_mix = ΔH_mix − T ΔS_mix ≈ −T ΔS_mix | Vapor pressure follows Raoult's law exactly; freezing/boiling point changes proportional to molality (constant K_f, K_b) | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Non-Ideal Solution (Positive Deviation)** | Intermolecular forces between unlike molecules weaker than between like molecules (repulsive); molecules "prefer" own kind | P_measured > P_ideal (Raoult's); system minimizes intermolecular contacts → less attractive interactions | ΔH_mix > 0 (endothermic mixing, requires heat to mix); may form azeotrope at specific composition | Example: CS₂ + acetone (both nonpolar but poor compatibility); benzene + alcohol (H-bonding disrupted) | 2022, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Non-Ideal Solution (Negative Deviation)** | Intermolecular forces between unlike molecules stronger than between like molecules (attractive); solute-solvent interactions favored | P_measured < P_ideal (Raoult's); strong attractions reduce escaping tendency → lower VP | ΔH_mix < 0 (exothermic mixing, releases heat); favorable interactions | Example: water + HCl (strong H-bonding between water & HCl); water + ethanol (H-bonding network) | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |

## SECTION 8: COMMON PITFALLS & ERROR PREVENTION

### Table 8.1: Typical Errors & Prevention Tactics

| Error | Why It Occurs | Consequence | Prevention Tactic | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Forgetting van 't Hoff Factor (i)** | Assumes i=1 for all solutes (only true for non-electrolytes) | Predicted ΔT_f, ΔT_b, π off by factor of 2−3 (very wrong for salts); method fails | Always determine if solute dissociates; i_NaCl ≈ 1.86 (not 2); i_CaCl₂ ≈ 2.7 | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Confusing Molality vs. Molarity** | Both "M" symbol sometimes used; one uses solvent mass, other solution volume | ΔT = K_f × m (molality required); if use M by mistake, error ~5−10% for dilute aqueous | Always check: molality = mol/kg solvent; molarity = mol/L solution; FPD/BPE use molality | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Using Mole Fraction instead of Molality for ΔT** | Raoult's law uses mole fraction for VP; but FPD uses molality (different bases) | Completely wrong answer; ΔP proportional to χ but ΔT_f to m (molality); off by orders of magnitude | ΔP (vapor pressure) uses χ; ΔT (freezing/boiling) uses m (molality); memorize which formula uses which concentration unit | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Miscalculating K_sp for AB₂ or A₂B Salts** | Only knows K_sp = S² (1:1 salt); forgets stoichiometry for other ratios | For PbCl₂: K_sp = 4S³ not S²; solubility calculation off by orders of magnitude | For AB₂: [A²⁺] = S, [B⁻] = 2S → K_sp = S × (2S)² = 4S³; for A₂B: similar (4S³); always write dissociation first | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Ignoring Common Ion Effect** | Calculates solubility as if no other ions present | In 0.1 M NaCl, AgCl solubility much lower (~10⁻⁹ M, not 10⁻⁵ M); prediction catastrophically wrong | Always ask: are there common ions in solution? If yes, use K_sp = [cation]_total × [anion]_free (not just from salt) | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Assuming Ideal Solution When Real Data Given** | Doesn't check if Raoult's law holds; assumes V_total = V_A + V_B (additive) | Incorrect VP prediction; wrong distillation composition; azeotrope behavior missed | If problem gives "negative deviation" or "positive deviation" or "volume change," solution is non-ideal; use activity coefficients (γ ≠ 1) | 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Wrong Sign on Solubility Temperature Dependence** | Forgets endothermic (↑S with T) vs. exothermic (↓S with T) effects | Predicts KNO₃ solubility decreases with heating (completely wrong; should triple from 0 to 100°C) | Endothermic dissolution: ΔH_sol > 0 → S increases with T (most salts); Exothermic: ΔH_sol < 0 → S decreases with T (gases, some hydroxides) | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐ |
