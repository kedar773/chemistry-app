## SECTION 3: CHEMICAL PROPERTIES

### 3.1 Degree of Unsaturation (DBE) / Unsaturation Index

| Formula | DBE Calculation | Meaning | Examples | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (Saturated alkane)** | DBE = 0 | No unsaturation (all single bonds) | Alkanes | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| **CₙH₂ₙ (One unsaturation)** | DBE = 1 | One double bond OR one ring | Alkenes, Cycloalkanes | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| **CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ (Two unsaturations)** | DBE = 2 | Two double bonds, one triple bond, OR two rings | Dienes, Alkynes, Cycloalkenes | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| **C₆H₆ (Benzene)** | DBE = 4 | 3 double bonds + aromatic resonance | Benzene, simple aromatics | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**DBE = (2C + 2 - H) / 2** for hydrocarbon CₙHₓ  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced
