|---|---|---|---|

## SECTION 3: CHEMICAL PROPERTIES - BASICITY

### 3.1 Basicity Order & pKb Values

#### 3.1.1 Aliphatic Amines (Aqueous Solution)

| Amine | pKb | Relative Basicity | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Methyl amine (CH₃NH₂)** | 3.36 | Strongest aliphatic | Good electron donation |
| **Ethyl amine (C₂H₅NH₂)** | 3.25 | — | Similar to methylamine |
| **Dimethyl amine ((CH₃)₂NH)** | 3.27 | **STRONGEST among aliphatic** | Optimal +I effect, lone pair availability |
| **Trimethyl amine ((CH₃)₃N)** | 4.19 | Weak (in aqueous) | Solvation effect (large, hydrophobic) |
| **Aniline (C₆H₅NH₂)** | 9.37 | **WEAKEST** | Resonance deactivation |

**BASICITY ORDER (Aliphatic, Aqueous):**
**Dimethyl amine > Methyl amine ≈ Ethyl amine > Trimethyl amine**

#### 3.1.2 Aromatic vs Aliphatic Comparison

| Type | Compound | pKb | Basicity Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aliphatic 1°** | CH₃NH₂ | 3.36 | Strong |
| **Aliphatic 2°** | (CH₃)₂NH | 3.27 | **Strongest** |
| **Aliphatic 3°** | (CH₃)₃N | 4.19 | Weak (aqueous) |
| **Aromatic** | C₆H₅NH₂ (Aniline) | 9.37 | **Very weak** |

**Key Difference:** Aliphatic 2° >> Aromatic 1° (resonance in aniline weakens lone pair)

#### 3.1.3 Aniline & Substituted Anilines

| Compound | pKb | Basicity | Substituent Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aniline (C₆H₅NH₂)** | 9.37 | Weak | Baseline |
| **2-Methylaniline (o-Toluidine)** | 8.92 | Stronger than aniline | **+CH₃ (EDG) increases basicity** |
| **4-Methylaniline (p-Toluidine)** | 8.92 | Stronger than aniline | **+CH₃ (EDG) increases basicity** |
| **4-Methoxyaniline** | 8.36 | Stronger | **+OCH₃ (EDG) increases basicity** |
| **4-Nitroaniline (p-Nitroaniline)** | 13.0 | **VERY weak** | **-NO₂ (EWG) decreases basicity** |
| **4-Chloroaniline** | 9.65 | Weaker than aniline | **-Cl (EWG, slight) decreases basicity** |

**Trend:**
- **Electron-donating groups (alkyl, alkoxy) → MORE BASIC**
- **Electron-withdrawing groups (NO₂, CN) → LESS BASIC**

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 3.2 Basicity in Non-Aqueous Solvents

| Amine | Basicity in Aqueous | Basicity in Non-Aqueous (Gas Phase) | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trimethyl amine** | Weakest (aqueous) | **STRONGEST (gas phase)** | Solvation disadvantage in water (large, hydrophobic) |
| **Dimethyl amine** | Strongest (aqueous) | Moderate (gas phase) | Good solvation in water |
| **Methyl amine** | Strong | Moderate-weak | More solvated in water |

**Key Point:** In aqueous solution, solvation energy is critical  
**Year Tags:** 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

## SECTION 4: CHARACTERISTIC REACTIONS & TESTS

### 4.1 Distinguishing Tests

| Test Name | Reactant | Reagent | Observation | Compound Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbylamine Test** | R-NH₂ (1°) | CHCl₃ + KOH (alcoholic) | **Foul-smelling (mustard oil)** | Primary amines only |
| **Hinsberg Test** | All amines | C₆H₅SO₂Cl + pyridine | Solubility pattern | 1°, 2°, 3° distinction |
| **Azo Coupling** | Ar-NH₂ | After diazotization + 1-naphthol + NaOH | **Orange-yellow dye (Aniline yellow)** | Aromatic 1° amines |
| **Lassaigne's Test** | Any N compound | Fuse with Na, then Fe³⁺ | Blood-red color (Prussian blue if acidified) | Presence of N |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 4.2 Nitro Compound Characteristic Properties

| Property | Aromatic Nitro (Ar-NO₂) | Aliphatic Nitro (R-NO₂) |
|---|---|---|
| **Polarity** | Polar (electron-withdrawing) | Polar |
| **Acidity (α-H)** | Very weak (aromatic H not acidic) | Weakly acidic (pKa ~10-11) |
| **Reduction** | Sn/HCl → Aniline (easy) | Sn/HCl → Aliphatic amine |
| **Resonance** | Deactivates benzene ring (meta-directing) | Not applicable |
| **Color** | Often yellow/brown (aromatic) | Colorless or pale |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 4.3 Nitrile (Cyanide) Properties

| Property | Characteristic | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Polarity** | Polar (C≡N triple bond) | C≡N slightly polar |
| **Boiling Point** | Higher than aldehydes/ketones of similar M.W. | Sp hybridized carbon |
| **Hydrolysis** | Easy (R-CN → R-COOH) | Requires heat + acid/base |
| **Reduction** | R-CN → R-CH₂-NH₂ (Primary amine only) | LiAlH₄ or H₂/catalyst |
| **Acidity (α-H)** | Very weak (pKa ~25) | CH₂ not acidic |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced
