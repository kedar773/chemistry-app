## SECTION 5: COMPARISON TABLE

| Hydrocarbon Type | Key Reactions | Selectivity/Conditions | Products |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alkanes** | Free radical halogenation, combustion | 3° > 2° > 1° (for Br) | Alkyl halides |
| **Alkenes** | Electrophilic addition (HX, X₂, H₂O), ozonolysis | Markovnikov or anti-Markovnikov | Alcohols, dihalides, diols, carbonyl compounds |
| **Alkynes** | Similar to alkenes, but 2 addition equivalents | Markovnikov for HX | Alkenes (partial), alkanes (full) |
| **Aromatics** | EAS (halogenation, nitration, acylation), SNAr | Ortho/para (activating) or meta (deactivating) | Substituted benzenes |
