## SECTION 0: KÖSSEL-LEWIS APPROACH, FORMAL CHARGE & OCTET EXCEPTIONS

| Concept / Category | Definition / Formula | Mechanism / Key Rules | Representative Examples | Limitations / Notes | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Formal Charge ($FC$)** | $FC = V - L - \frac{1}{2}S$ where $V = \text{valence } e^-$, $L = \text{lone pair } e^-$, $S = \text{shared } e^-$ | Minimizes formal charge magnitude; negative charge preferred on more electronegative atom | Ozone ($\text{O}_3$): Central $\text{O} = +1$, end $\text{O} = 0, -1$ | Formal charge is a bookkeeping tool, not true charge | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Incomplete Octet (Hypovalent)** | Central atom surrounded by fewer than $8$ valence electrons | Highly reactive electrophiles; accept electron pairs to form coordinate bonds | $\text{BeCl}_2$ ($4e^-$), $\text{BF}_3$ ($6e^-$), $\text{AlCl}_3$ ($6e^-$) | Readily act as **Lewis acids** ($\text{BF}_3 + \text{NH}_3 \rightarrow \text{F}_3\text{B}\leftarrow\text{NH}_3$) | 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025 | ⭐ |
| **Expanded Octet (Hypervalent)** | Central atom possesses more than $8$ valence electrons | Utilizes vacant low-energy $d$-orbitals; elements in Period 3 and beyond | $\text{PCl}_5$ ($10e^-$), $\text{SF}_6$ ($12e^-$), $\text{IF}_7$ ($14e^-$), $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ | First-row elements (C, N, O, F) **cannot** expand octet ($d$-orbitals absent) | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Odd-Electron Molecules** | Total valence electron count is an odd integer | Incomplete octet on nitrogen; inherently paramagnetic and undergo dimerization | $\text{NO}$ ($11e^-$), $\text{NO}_2$ ($17e^-$), $\text{ClO}_2$ ($19e^-$) | $\text{NO}_2$ dimerizes to diamagnetic $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4$ at lower temperatures | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
