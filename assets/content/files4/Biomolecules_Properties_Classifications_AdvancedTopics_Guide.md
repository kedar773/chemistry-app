## SECTION 2: PROTEIN CLASSIFICATION — FIBROUS VS. GLOBULAR

| Parameter / Feature | Fibrous Proteins (Structural) | Globular Proteins (Functional) | Chemical Rationale | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Polypeptide Chain Shape** | Long, linear parallel thread-like fiber chains | Folded tightly into spherical/ellipsoidal 3D shapes | Hydrophobic residues pack inside core; polar residues on surface | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Intermolecular Forces** | Strong intermolecular hydrogen bonds and covalent **disulfide ($-\text{S-S}-$) crosslinks** | Weak van der Waals forces, internal H-bonds, salt bridges, dipole-dipole | Strong cross-linking prevents solvent penetration | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 | ⭐ |
| **Solubility in Water** | **Insoluble in water** | **Soluble in water** (forms colloidal solutions) | Hydrophilic polar side chains exposed to aqueous environment | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Stability to pH & Temperature** | Highly stable and resilient | Highly sensitive to small changes in temperature and pH (denatures readily) | Delicate tertiary active sites deform when weak interactions break | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Representative Examples** | **Keratin** (hair, wool, nails, horns), **Myosin** (muscles), **Collagen** (tendons, skin), **Fibroin** (silk) | **Insulin** (hormone), **Albumin** (egg white), **Haemoglobin** (oxygen transport), **All Enzymes** | Structural vs metabolic functions in living organisms | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |

## SECTION 3: FOUR STRUCTURAL LEVELS OF PROTEINS & DENATURATION

| Structural Level | Definition / Geometry | Stabilizing Chemical Bonds / Interactions | Effect of Denaturation | Real-World Example | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Structure ($1^\circ$)** | Specific linear sequence of amino acids linked in a polypeptide chain | **Covalent peptide bonds ($-\text{CONH}-$)** exclusively | **Remains intact** (covalent bonds do not break under mild heat/acid) | Sequence of 51 amino acids in human insulin | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Secondary Structure ($2^\circ$)** | Regular local folding of polypeptide backbone: **$\alpha$-Helix** and **$\beta$-Pleated Sheet** | **Intramolecular Hydrogen bonds** between $>\text{C}=\text{O}$ and $-\text{NH}-$ of peptide backbone | **Completely disrupted / uncoiled** | $\alpha$-keratin in hair ($\alpha$-helix); fibroin in silk ($\beta$-sheet) | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Tertiary Structure ($3^\circ$)** | Overall 3D spatial conformation caused by extensive folding and looping | Hydrogen bonds, **disulfide bridges ($-\text{S-S}-$)**, ionic salt bridges, hydrophobic interactions | **Completely destroyed**; loss of biological enzymatic activity | Active catalytic site of enzymes; compact globular shape | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Quaternary Structure ($4^\circ$)** | Spatial assembly of two or more independent polypeptide subunit chains | Non-covalent interactions (hydrophobic, electrostatic, H-bonds) between subunits | **Subunits dissociate**; complete loss of cooperative function | Haemoglobin (tetramer composed of $2\alpha$ and $2\beta$ polypeptide chains) | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
