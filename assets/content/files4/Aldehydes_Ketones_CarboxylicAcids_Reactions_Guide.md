## SECTION 1: ALDEHYDE REACTIONS

### 1.1 Oxidation Reactions

#### 1.1.1 Mild Oxidation (Aldehyde to Carboxylic Acid)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-CHO | K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄ | H₂SO₄, RT to mild heat | R-COOH | — |
| R-CHO | KMnO₄ | Acidic, cold | R-COOH | — |
| R-CHO | HNO₃(dilute) | Cold/RT | R-COOH | — |
| R-CHO | Ag(NH₃)₂⁺ (Tollens) | Alkaline, RT | R-COO⁻Ag⁺ + Ag(mirror) | — |
| R-CHO | Cu(II)/Benedict's reagent | Heat in aqueous base | R-COOH + Cu₂O(brick-red ppt) | — |

**Silver Mirror Test (Tollens Test):** Gives silver mirror with aldehydes only (not ketones)  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.1.2 Aldehyde to Ketone (Etard Reaction - Not Common)

|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-------|

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 1.2 Reduction Reactions

#### 1.2.1 Aldehyde to Primary Alcohol

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-CHO | LiAlH₄ | Dry ether, RT | R-CH₂-OH | — |
| R-CHO | NaBH₄ | EtOH or aqueous, RT | R-CH₂-OH | — |
| R-CHO | DIBAL-H (Diisobutylaluminum hydride) | -78°C, ethereal | R-CH₂-OH | Partial reduction (stops at aldehyde) |
| R-CHO | Zn-Hg/conc. HCl (Clemmensen) | Heat, acidic | R-CH₃ | Complete deoxygenation |
| R-CHO | NH₂-NH₂/KOH, glycol (Wolff-Kishner) | Heat (100-200°C), basic | R-CH₃ | Complete deoxygenation |
| R-CHO | H₂/Ni catalyst | Heat, pressure | R-CH₂-OH | Mild conditions |
| R-CHO | H₂/Pt or Pd catalyst | RT to heat | R-CH₂-OH | — |

**Clemmensen Reduction (Zn-Hg/HCl):** Acidic conditions, complete reduction to CH₃  
**Wolff-Kishner Reduction (NH₂-NH₂/KOH):** Basic conditions, complete reduction to CH₃  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.2.2 Aldehyde Partial Reduction

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-CHO (α,β-unsaturated) | DIBAL-H | -78°C, ethereal, quick workup | R-CH=CH-CH₂-OH | Allylic alcohol (partial reduction) |
| Ar-CHO | Rosenmund Reduction: H₂/Lindlar Pd + BaSO₄ | RT, pressure | Ar-CHO (Ar-CHO + trace Ar-CH₂-OH) | Partial reduction |

**Rosenmund Reduction:** H₂ with poisoned (BaSO₄) catalyst to prevent over-reduction  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.3.2 Addition of Alcohol (Acetal/Hemiacetal Formation)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-CHO | 1 R'OH | H⁺ cat., RT, no heat | R-CH(OH)-O-R' (Hemiacetal) | — |

**Hemiacetal:** Intermediate, unstable, formed at RT  
**Acetal:** Stable, formed with excess alcohol + heat + acid catalyst  
**Use:** Protecting group for aldehyde  
**Year Tags:** 2023, 2022, 2021

| HCHO + 2 R-MgX | R-MgX | Dry ether | H-C(OH)(R)₂ (Tertiary alcohol) | — |

### 1.4 Condensation Reactions

#### 1.4.1 Aldol Condensation (with aldehydes)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Products | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 2 R-CHO (same) | Base (NaOH, KOH, KCN, etc.) | Aqueous/alcoholic, RT to mild heat | R-CH(OH)-CH₂-CHO (Aldol product) | Mixed stereoisomers possible |
| R-CHO + R-CHO (same) | Base | RT then heat | R-CH=CH-CHO (α,β-unsaturated aldehyde) | Dehydration product |
| R-CHO (with α-H) + Ar-CHO (no α-H) | Base | RT | R-CH₂-CH(Ar)-CHO (Cross aldol) | — |

**Aldol Intermediate:** R-CH(OH)-CH₂-CHO (unstable, eliminates water on heating)  
**Final Product:** α,β-Unsaturated aldehyde (Crotonaldehyde from acetaldehyde)  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.4.2 Cannizzaro Reaction (for Formaldehyde)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Products | Notes |
|----------|---------|-----------|----------|-------|
| 2 HCHO (Formaldehyde, no α-H) | Strong base (NaOH, KOH) | Concentrated, heat | CH₃-OH (Methanol) + HCO₂⁻Na⁺ (Formate) | Disproportionation |
| 2 Ar-CHO (no α-H, like benzaldehyde) | Concentrated KOH | RT or mild heat | Ar-CH₂-OH (Benzyl alcohol) + Ar-COO⁻K⁺ (Benzoate) | Cannizzaro reaction |

**Conditions:** Formaldehyde has no α-H → Cannot do aldol → Undergoes disproportionation  
**Year Tags:** 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.4.3 Perkin Reaction

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Ar-CHO (Benzaldehyde) | CH₂(COOR)₂ + base | Acetic anhydride, heat | Ar-CH=C(COOR)₂ (α,β-unsaturated ester) | Condensation with active CH₂ |

**Year Tags:** 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

## SECTION 2: KETONE REACTIONS

### 2.1 Oxidation Reactions

#### 2.1.1 Strong Oxidation (C-C Bond Cleavage)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Products | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| R₁-CO-R₂ | KMnO₄ | Acidic, heat | R₁-COOH + R₂-COOH (or CO₂) | — |
| R₁-CO-R₂ | K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄ | Heat, acidic | R₁-COOH + R₂-COOH | — |
| CH₃-CO-R | KMnO₄ | Acidic/Neutral | CH₃-COOH + R-COOH | Methyl ketones |
| R₁-CO-R₂ (with benzylic position) | CrO₂Cl₂ (Chromyl chloride) | CCl₄, RT | Oxidation products | Selective oxidation |

**Ketones are MORE resistant to oxidation than aldehydes**  
**Mild oxidizing agents don't oxidize ketones**  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 2.2 Reduction Reactions

#### 2.2.1 Ketone to Secondary Alcohol

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R₁-CO-R₂ (chiral at C) | LiAlH₄ | Dry ether, RT or -78°C | R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ (Secondary alcohol) | **Inversion if C is stereogenic** |
| R₁-CO-R₂ (chiral at C) | NaBH₄ | EtOH or aqueous, RT | R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ (Secondary alcohol) | **Inversion if C is stereogenic** |
| R₁-CO-R₂ | DIBAL-H | -78°C, ethereal | R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ (slow, partial reduction) | — |
| R₁-CO-R₂ | H₂/Ni catalyst | Heat, pressure | R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ (Secondary alcohol) | — |

**Stereochemistry:** LiAlH₄ and NaBH₄ show inversion if stereogenic center at carbonyl carbon  
**Prelog's Rule:** Hydride attacks from less hindered face (anti to larger group)  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

| R₁-CO-R₂ | NH₂-NH₂/KOH, glycol (Wolff-Kishner) | Heat (100-200°C), basic | R₁-CH₂-R₂ | — |

**Clemmensen Reduction:** Acidic, works well for aliphatic and aromatic ketones  
**Wolff-Kishner Reduction:** Basic, complementary to Clemmensen

## SECTION 3: CARBOXYLIC ACID REACTIONS

### 3.1 Esterification Reactions

#### 3.1.1 Fischer Esterification (Alcohol + Acid)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-COOH + R'-OH | H₂SO₄(conc.) | Reflux, Dean-Stark trap (water removal) | R-COO-R' (Ester) + H₂O | — |
| R-COOH + R'-OH | H⁺ (acid catalyst) | Heat | R-COO-R' (Ester) | — |
| Ar-COOH + R-OH | H₂SO₄ or p-TsOH | Heat | Ar-COO-R (Aryl ester) | — |

**Equilibrium:** Reversible reaction, shifted by removing water  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 3.1.2 Acid Chloride Formation & Esterification

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-COOH | SOCl₂ | RT or mild heat | R-COCl (Acid chloride) + SO₂↑ + HCl↑ | — |
| R-COOH | PCl₃ or PCl₅ | Heat | R-COCl (Acid chloride) + HCl/POCl₃ | — |
| R-COCl (intermediate) | R'-OH | RT (exothermic, no catalyst needed) | R-COO-R' (Ester) | — |

**Via Acid Chloride:** Much faster esterification (no equilibrium, irreversible)  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

| (R-CO)₂O (intermediate) | R'-OH | RT (exothermic) | R-COO-R' (Ester) | — |

**Year Tags:** 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 3.2 Reduction Reactions

#### 3.2.1 Carboxylic Acid to Aldehyde

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Stereochemistry |
|----------|---------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| R-COOH | DIBAL-H (Diisobutylaluminum hydride) | -78°C, ethereal, quick workup | R-CHO (Aldehyde) + partial R-CH₂-OH | Partial reduction |
| R-COOH | LiAlH₄ (in 2 steps, stoichiometric control) | -78°C, then careful workup | R-CHO (minor) + R-CH₂-OH (major) | — |

**DIBAL-H:** Best for selective reduction to aldehyde (stop at RCHO)  
**LiAlH₄:** Over-reduces to alcohol (primary product)  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 3.2.2 Carboxylic Acid to Primary Alcohol

## SECTION 6: IMPORTANT STEREOCHEMICAL ASPECTS

### 6.1 Aldehyde/Ketone Reduction - Stereochemistry

| Reaction | Mechanism | Stereochemistry | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| **LiAlH₄ reduction of chiral aldehyde/ketone** | Nu-addition | **Inversion** (Prelog rule) | Hydride from less hindered face |
| **NaBH₄ reduction of chiral aldehyde/ketone** | Nu-addition | **Inversion** (Prelog rule) | Similar to LiAlH₄ |
| **Grignard addition to chiral aldehyde/ketone** | Nu-addition | **Inversion** (Prelog rule) | R-MgX attacks from less hindered |

**Prelog's Rule:** For C=O with two different alkyl groups, new alcohol is formed by hydride/nucleophile attacking from face opposite to larger group

### 6.2 Cyanohydrin Formation - Stereochemistry
