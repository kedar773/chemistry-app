## SECTION 1: CRYSTAL FIELD THEORY BASICS

| Concept | Octahedral (d^n Examples) | Tetrahedral | Square Planar (d⁸, d⁹) | Physical Impact | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---------|--------------------------|-----------|----------------------|-----------------|----------|-----------|
| **d-Orbital Splitting (Octahedral)** | t₂g (3 orbitals, lower) vs e_g (2 orbitals, higher); Δ_o = energy gap | Δ_t ≈ 4/9 Δ_o (inverse level order: e > t₂) | Square planar (larger d_x²-y² splitting) | Orbital energy differences determine electron occupation, affecting magnetism & color | 2023–2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Crystal Field Stabilization Energy (CFSE)** | Depends on d^n & Δ_o; t₂g⁶ (low-spin d⁶) has maximum stability (all t₂g filled, no e_g population) | d¹ (t₂g¹, CFSE = −0.4Δ_o); d⁶ LS (t₂g⁶, CFSE = −1.2Δ_o) | d⁸ square planar has high CFSE | Larger CFSE favors low-spin (strong-field ligands); determines complex geometry & stability | 2024–2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **High-Spin vs Low-Spin (d⁴, d⁵, d⁶, d⁷, d⁸)** | HS: all spins parallel (Hund's rule, weak field Δ_o < pairing energy P); LS: paired electrons (strong field Δ_o > P) | High-spin d⁶: ↑↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ ↑ (4 unpaired); low-spin d⁶: ↑↓ ↑↓ ↑↓ (0 unpaired) | d⁸ must be low-spin in octahedral (can't be paramagnetic HS); square planar always low-spin | Ligand field strength (CN⁻ > CO > NH₃ > H₂O > Cl⁻) determines HS/LS; affects color & magnetism | 2021–2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Color Prediction (CFT Basis)** | Color λ ∝ 1/Δ_o: larger Δ_o → shorter λ (blue-shifted); smaller Δ_o → longer λ (red-shifted) | [Fe(H₂O)₆]²⁺ pale green (weak Δ_o); [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ yellow (large Δ_o strong-field CN⁻) | d⁹ (Cu²⁺) intense blue (one d-hole); split t₂g⁶ e_g³ causes strong absorption | Qualitative: weak-field complexes pale colored; strong-field complexes intense colors (more d-d transitions in visible) | 2021–2026 | ⭐⭐ |

## SECTION 8: FINAL JEE MASTERY CHECKLIST

✅ **Electronic Configuration**: Recognize Cr ([Ar]3d⁵4s¹) & Cu ([Ar]3d¹⁰4s¹) anomalies; understand half-filled d⁵ & full d¹⁰ preference  
✅ **Oxidation States**: +2 & +3 most common (4s removed first); predict via electron removal sequence  
✅ **Color Prediction**: d⁰ & d¹⁰ colorless; d¹-d⁹ colored (d-d transitions); intensity ∝ Δ_o (ligand field strength)  
✅ **Magnetism**: Calculate μ = √[n(n+2)] BM for high-spin; d⁵ always high-spin; d⁶ can be HS or LS  
✅ **Complex Stability**: K_f huge for polydentate (chelate effect ~10,000×); EDTA forms most stable complexes (~10²⁴-10²⁵)  
✅ **Redox Chemistry**: Use E° to predict spontaneity (ΔG° = −nFE°); higher E° = easier to reduce = stronger oxidizer  
✅ **Catalysis**: Heterogeneous (surface): identify bond activation step; homogeneous (complex): trace redox cycle  
✅ **Lanthanide Contraction**: ~30 pm across 4f; causes 4d ≈ 5d; explains element separation & d-block trends  
✅ **Industrial Processes**: Haber (N₂ + H₂ ⇌ NH₃, Fe catalyst), Contact (SO₂ + O₂ → SO₃, V₂O₅ catalyst), Ostwald (NH₃ → NO → HNO₃, Pt catalyst)
