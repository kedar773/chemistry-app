## QUICK REFERENCE: REACTIVITY ORDER & KEY MECHANISMS

### Haloalkane SN2 Reactivity (with OH⁻)
**Reactivity: Primary >> Secondary >> Tertiary (steric hindrance)**
- Primary: SN2 dominant, fast
- Secondary: Mixed SN1/SN2
- Tertiary: E1/SN1, elimination major

### Halogenation Reactivity Order (Nucleophilicity)
**Nu⁻ Reactivity in Aprotic Solvents: I⁻ > Br⁻ > Cl⁻ > F⁻** (reverse of protic)

### Haloarene Reactivity (SNAr)
**Reactivity: F > Cl > Br > I** (reverse of haloalkanes; EAS-driven)

### EAS on Haloarenes: Directing Effects
- **Halogens = Ortho/Para Directors (deactivating)**
- Reduced reactivity vs. benzene; requires forcing conditions
- Lone pairs on X (resonance donation) weak; -I effect dominates
