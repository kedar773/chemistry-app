## SECTION 3: INDUCTIVE & RESONANCE EFFECTS

### 3.1: Inductive Effect (Electron-Withdrawing & Electron-Donating Groups)

| Group Type | Symbol | Effect | Mechanism | Acidity Impact | Basicity Impact | Example | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|---------|-----------||-----------|
| **Electron-Withdrawing (–I effect)** | -I | Withdraws electrons through σ-bonds | Pulls electron density away from adjacent atom/bond | Increases acidity (stabilizes negative charge) | Decreases basicity (reduces lone pair availability) | -Cl, -Br, -NO₂, -CN, -COR | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Electron-Donating (+I effect)** | +I | Donates electrons through σ-bonds | Pushes electron density toward adjacent atom/bond | Decreases acidity (destabilizes negative charge) | Increases basicity (enhances lone pair) | -CH₃, -C₂H₅, -OH (O is electronegative but +I from C-O), amines | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Halogen as -I Effect** | -I | Withdraws (despite lone pairs; -I dominates in σ-framework) | Electronegativity > resonance donation | Makes attached group more acidic | Deactivates lone pair | -F >> -Cl > -Br > -I (in aliphatic) | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Distance Effect** | Distance | -I effect decreases with distance from substituent | Through-bond propagation weakens over distance | Closer EWG → stronger effect | Closer EWG → stronger deactivation | 2-haloethanol vs. 3-haloethanol | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ |

### 3.2: Resonance Effect (Electron-Donating & Electron-Withdrawing by π-Systems)

| Group Type | Symbol | Effect | Mechanism | Acidity Impact | Basicity Impact | Example | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|---------|-----------||-----------|
| **Electron-Donating by Resonance** | R (or +R) | Donates through π-system (delocalization) | Lone pair on atom conjugated with π-bond or aromatic ring | Decreases acidity (destabilizes negative charge; lone pair delocalized) | Increases basicity (π-system enhances lone pair) | -OR (alkoxy), -NR₂ (amino), -OH, -OAr on benzene | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
