## SECTION 2: FAJAN'S RULES — COVALENT CHARACTER IN IONIC COMPOUNDS

| Factor Governing Covalent Character | Rule Statement | High Covalent Character Condition | Low Covalent Character (Predominantly Ionic) | Direct Physical Consequences (Melting Point & Solubility) | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cation Size** | Smaller cation has higher charge density and stronger polarizing power | **Small cation** (e.g., $\text{Li}^+ > \text{Na}^+ > \text{K}^+$) | Large cation (e.g., $\text{Cs}^+$) | $\text{LiCl}$ is covalent and soluble in organic solvents; $\text{NaCl}$ is ionic | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Anion Size** | Larger anion has loosely held valence shell and higher polarizability | **Large anion** (e.g., $\text{I}^- > \text{Br}^- > \text{Cl}^- > \text{F}^-$) | Small anion (e.g., $\text{F}^-$) | $\text{AlI}_3$ is predominantly covalent; $\text{AlF}_3$ is predominantly ionic (high m.p.) | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Charge on Ions** | Higher charge on cation or anion exponentially increases polarization | **High ionic charge** ($\text{Sn}^{4+} > \text{Sn}^{2+}$; $\text{Fe}^{3+} > \text{Fe}^{2+}$) | Low ionic charge ($\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+$) | $\text{SnCl}_4$ is a covalent liquid (b.p. $114^\circ\text{C}$); $\text{SnCl}_2$ is an ionic solid (m.p. $247^\circ\text{C}$) | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Electronic Configuration of Cation** | Pseudo-noble gas configuration ($18e^-$ core: $ns^2np^6nd^{10}$) polarizes far more effectively than inert gas core ($8e^-$: $ns^2np^6$) | **Pseudo-inert core** ($\text{Cu}^+, \text{Ag}^+, \text{Zn}^{2+}, \text{Cd}^{2+}$) | Inert gas core ($\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+, \text{Ca}^{2+}$) | $\text{CuCl}$ is far more covalent than $\text{NaCl}$ ($\text{CuCl}$ m.p. $426^\circ\text{C}$ vs $\text{NaCl}$ m.p. $801^\circ\text{C}$) | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

## SECTION 3: HYDROGEN BONDING — INTER VS. INTRAMOLECULAR

| Type of H-Bonding | Structural Requirement | Key Chemical Phenomenon / Effect | Benchmark Examples | Comparison / Exam Pitfalls | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Intermolecular H-Bonding** | Occurs between distinct separate molecules; requires $\text{H}$ attached to $\text{F, O, N}$ | Causes **molecular association**, drastically raises boiling points, increases viscosity and water solubility | $\text{H}_2\text{O}, \text{HF}, \text{NH}_3, \text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$, p-nitrophenol | High boiling points; higher melting points; non-volatile with steam | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐ |
| **Intramolecular H-Bonding (Chelation)** | Occurs within the same molecule; forms a stable 5- or 6-membered ring | **Prevents association** with neighboring molecules; lowers boiling point, increases volatility and lipid solubility | **o-Nitrophenol**, Salicylaldehyde, Salicylic acid | **Steam volatile**; easily separated from p-nitrophenol by steam distillation | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Anomalous Properties of Water & Ice** | Extensive 3D tetrahedral cage structure of H-bonds in solid ice | **Ice has lower density than liquid water**; maximum density of water occurs at **$4^\circ\text{C}$** | Ice floats on water; preserves aquatic life during winter freezes | Cage-like open lattice collapses upon melting from $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $4^\circ\text{C}$ | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐ |
| **Strength Comparison of Hydrides** | Electronegativity of heteroatom: $\text{F} > \text{O} > \text{N}$ | Hydrogen bond strength order: $\mathbf{\text{H}\cdots\text{F} > \text{H}\cdots\text{O} > \text{H}\cdots\text{N}}$ | $\text{HF}$ has strongest single H-bond, but $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ has higher boiling point ($100^\circ\text{C}$ vs $19.5^\circ\text{C}$) | $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ forms **4 H-bonds per molecule** on average, whereas $\text{HF}$ forms only 2 | 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 | ⭐⭐ |

## SECTION 4: INTERMOLECULAR (VAN DER WAALS) FORCES

| Force Type | Interacting Species | Potential Energy Distance Dependence | Typical Energy Magnitude | Physical Significance / Examples | PYQ Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ion - Dipole** | Ion + Polar Molecule | $V \propto -\frac{1}{r^2}$ | $40 - 600 \text{ kJ/mol}$ | Hydration of ions in aqueous solutions: $\text{Na}^+(aq) + \text{H}_2\text{O}$ | 2021, 2023, 2024 | ⭐ |
| **Dipole - Dipole (Keesom)** | Two Polar Molecules (Stationary) | $V \propto -\frac{1}{r^3}$ | $5 - 25 \text{ kJ/mol}$ | Alignment of permanent dipoles: $\text{HCl}\cdots\text{HCl}, \text{SO}_2\cdots\text{SO}_2$ | 2021, 2022, 2024 | ⭐ |
| **Dipole - Dipole (Rotating)** | Two Polar Molecules (Rotating in liquid/gas) | $V \propto -\frac{1}{r^6}$ | $1 - 10 \text{ kJ/mol}$ | Thermal tumbling weakens effective interaction | 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐⭐ |
| **Dipole - Induced Dipole (Debye)** | Polar Molecule + Non-polar Molecule | $V \propto -\frac{1}{r^6}$ | $2 - 10 \text{ kJ/mol}$ | Solubility of non-polar gases in polar solvents: $\text{O}_2\text{ in }\text{H}_2\text{O}$, noble gases in water | 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 | ⭐ |
