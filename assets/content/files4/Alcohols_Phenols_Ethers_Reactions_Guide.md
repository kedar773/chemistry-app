| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Type of Reaction |

### 5.2 Cleavage Reactions

#### 5.2.1 Cleavage with HI (Hydriodic Acid)
| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Products | Mechanism | Selectivity |
|----------|---------|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| R-O-R' (Simple) | HI (aq) | Heat or RT | R-I + R'-OH | SN2 (via oxonium) | Less hindered C |
| C₂H₅-O-C₂H₅ | HI (excess) | Heat | 2 C₂H₅-I + H₂O | SN2 | Both alcohols oxidized |
| CH₃-O-R | HI (aq) | Heat | CH₃-I + R-OH | SN2 | Breaks at CH₃ |
| C₆H₅-O-R | HI (aq) | Heat | C₆H₅-OH + R-I | SN2 (Aryl-O not cleaved) | Breaks at alkyl |
| (CH₃)₃C-O-CH₃ | HI (aq) | Heat | (CH₃)₃C-I + CH₃-OH | SN1 | Less hindered |

**Stereochemistry:**
- If attacking carbon is chiral: **SN2 → Inversion**
- If carbocation intermediate: **SN1 → Racemization**

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 5.2.2 Cleavage with HBr (Hydrobromic Acid)

## SECTION 7: IMPORTANT STEREOCHEMICAL ASPECTS

### 7.1 Alcohol Reactions - Stereochemistry Summary

| Reaction Type | Reactant Stereochemistry | Mechanism | Product Stereochemistry |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oxidation** | (R)-R₂CH-OH | Inversion of configuration at Cr | (R) ketone (inversion at Cr) |
| **Dehydration (E1)** | (R) or (S) | Carbocation intermediate | Racemic/Mixed alkene |
| **PBr₃/SOCl₂** | (R)-R₂CH-OH | SN2 (backside attack) | **(S)-R₂CH-Br/Cl (Inversion)** |
| **HBr (SN1)** | (R)-R₂CH-OH | Carbocation | Racemic R₂CH-Br |
| **HBr + PBr₃** | (R)-R₂CH-OH | SN2 (PBr₃) → Inversion | (S)-R₂CH-Br |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Reactant | Nucleophile | Product | Stereochemistry |
|---|---|---|---|

## SECTION 9: VISUAL SUMMARY - REACTIVITY TRENDS

### Alcohol Oxidation Trends
```
Primary Alcohols:     R-CH₂-OH  →  R-CHO  →  R-COOH
                     (PCC)        (K₂Cr₂O₇)

Secondary Alcohols:   R₂CH-OH  →  R₂C=O
                     (Any oxidizing agent)

Tertiary Alcohols:    R₃C-OH  →  NO REACTION (mild conditions)
```

### Alcohol Dehydration Reactivity
```
3° > 2° > 1°
(Tertiary fastest, Primary slowest)
(E1 mechanism, Zaitsev's rule applies)
```

### Nucleophilic Substitution in Alcohols
```
SN2 (with PBr₃, SOCl₂):  
Mechanism: SN2 → Inversion
No carbocation rearrangement

SN1 (with HX):
Mechanism: SN1 → Racemization/Rearrangement
Reactivity: 3° >> 2° > 1°
```

### Phenol Halogenation Selectivity
```
C₆H₅-OH + Br₂/H₂O  →  2,4,6-Tribromophenol
(Polyhalogenation, no catalyst needed)
```

### Ether Cleavage Selectivity
```
R-O-R' + HI  →  Cleaves at LESS HINDERED C
            →  R-I + R'-OH  (or vice versa)
            
If excess HI: Alcohols further oxidized to alkyl halides
```
