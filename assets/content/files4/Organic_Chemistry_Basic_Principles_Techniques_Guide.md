|-----------|----------------|----------------------|--------------|----------|------------|-----------|-----------|

| **σ (Sigma) Bond (C-H)** | C-H single bond; sp³-s or sp²-s, sp-s | ~99 (C-H strongest) | Head-on overlap | Very strong | Unreactive under normal conditions | 2024, 2023 | ⭐⭐ |
| **π (Pi) Bond (C=C)** | Above/below σ framework; lateral overlap p-orbital | ~63 | Lateral p-p overlap | Weaker than σ | Easily broken; reactive (addition) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### 2.3: R/S Nomenclature (Stereogenic Priority)

| Priority Assignment | Rule | Highest Atomic # | Example Sequence | Application | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|-------------------|------|-----------------|------------------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Step 1: Atomic Number (1st Sphere)** | Assign 1-4 based on atomic # of atoms directly bonded | Cl > Br > C > H | C-Cl bond: Cl = #1 (highest); C-C bond: varies | Applied to all 4 groups at stereogenic center | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### 3.2: Reaction Mechanisms (Detailed)

#### 3.2.1: SN2 Mechanism (Nucleophilic Substitution, Bimolecular)

| Step | Process | Geometry | Stereochemistry | Rate | Activation Energy | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|------|---------|----------|-----------------|------|-------------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Step 1 (Only)** | Nu⁻ attacks from rear (backside) to C-X; simultaneous X⁻ departure | Inversion: attacks 180° opposite to leaving group | 100% inversion of stereochemistry | 2nd order (Rate = k[R-X][Nu⁻]) | Single transition state | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Transition State** | Pentavalent carbon (sp³→sp²); Nu-C-X linear | Trigonal bipyramidal arrangement | Max strain at TS | Determines overall rate | Highest energy point | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Product Geometry** | Return to sp³ with Nu in opposite position | Tetrahedral; inverted configuration | (R) → (S) or vice versa | Completed | - | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

#### 3.2.2: SN1 Mechanism (Nucleophilic Substitution, Unimolecular)

| Step | Process | Species | Carbocation Type | Rearrangement? | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|------|---------|---------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Step 1 (Slow)** | C-X bond ionization; heterolytic cleavage; carbocation formation | R⁺ + X⁻ (ionic pair) | 3° > 2° > 1° (stability determines rate) | Possible (hydride, alkyl shift if 2° cation can rearrange to 3°) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Step 2 (Fast)** | Nu⁻ attacks carbocation from both faces | Nu-C bond forms | Planar cation (sp²) attacked equally from both sides | After Nu⁻ adds, rearranged cation gives rearranged product | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Stereochemistry** | Mixture of inversion + retention (±50% each, or not equally if one face hindered) | Racemic if original optically active | Racemization (or partial if one face shielded) | Loss of stereochemical information | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

#### 3.2.3: E1 Mechanism (Elimination, Unimolecular)

| Step | Process | Carbocation | β-H Removal | Regioselectivity | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|------|---------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Step 1 (Slow)** | C-X ionization; carbocation formation | R⁺ intermediate | β-H accessible from carbocation | 3° > 2° (substrate dependent) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Step 2 (Fast)** | Base abstracts β-H; π-bond forms | Same carbocation (if rearrangement doesn't occur) | Weak bases OK (H₂O, EtOH, NO₃⁻); any β-H can be removed | **Zaitsev's rule: most substituted (stable) alkene** | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Rearrangement** | Carbocation may shift to more stable cation before β-H removal | 2° cation → 3° cation (hydride or alkyl shift) | Rearranged β-H removal possible | Rearranged + non-rearranged products | 2023, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ |

#### 3.2.4: E2 Mechanism (Elimination, Bimolecular)

| Step | Process | Geometry Requirement | Base Type | Regioselectivity | Stereochemistry | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|------|---------|---------------------|-----------|----------|----------|-----------|-----------|
