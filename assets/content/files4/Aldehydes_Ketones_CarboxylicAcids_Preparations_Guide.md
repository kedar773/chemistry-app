## SECTION 1: ALDEHYDE PREPARATION METHODS

### 1.1 From Primary Alcohols (Oxidation - Most Common)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Selectivity | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-CH₂-OH (1° Alcohol) | **PCC** (Pyridinium Chlorochromate) | CH₂Cl₂, RT | R-CHO (Aldehyde) | **Selective - Stops at aldehyde** | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **DMP** (Dess-Martin Periodinane) | CH₂Cl₂, RT | R-CHO | **Selective - Stops at aldehyde** | 2024, 2023 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **Jones Reagent** (CrO₃/H₂SO₄/Acetone) | RT | R-CHO | Fast reaction; tends to over-oxidize | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **Swern Oxidation** (COCl)₂/DMSO, -78°C then base | -78°C, then Et₃N | R-CHO | **Selective - Stops at aldehyde** | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **Oppenauer** (Reverse, Al(OiPr)₃ + aldehyde) | RT or mild heat | R-CHO | Mild conditions | 2021, 2020 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Selectivity Ranking:** **PCC/DMP/Swern > Oppenauer > Jones** (Jones over-oxidizes)  
**Best for JEE:** PCC or Swern (most selective to aldehyde)

### 1.2 From Acyl Chlorides (Reduction)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-COCl (Acyl chloride) | **H₂/Pd-BaSO₄** (Lindlar catalyst, **Rosenmund reduction**) | RT-120°C, moderate pressure | R-CHO (Aldehyde) | **BaSO₄ "poisons" Pd to prevent over-reduction** | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COCl | **DIBAL-H** | -78°C, ethereal, quick workup | R-CHO | Selective at low temperature | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COCl | **LiAlH₄** (stoichiometric, careful) | -78°C, strict control | R-CHO (minor) | Difficult to control; over-reduces to R-CH₂-OH | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Best Method:** Rosenmund reduction (H₂/Lindlar Pd + BaSO₄)

### 1.3 From Nitriles (Reduction)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Selectivity | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-C≡N (Nitrile) | **DIBAL-H** | -78°C, ethereal, quick workup, stoichiometric control | R-CHO (Aldehyde) | **Selective; requires careful control of equivalents** | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-C≡N | **H₂/Ni** (Raney nickel, partial) | Controlled pressure and temperature | R-CHO | Difficult to control stoichiometry | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐ Adv |
| R-C≡N | **LiAlH₄** | Standard conditions | R-CH₂-NH₂ (Amine) | Over-reduces; doesn't give aldehyde | — | — |

**Best Method:** DIBAL-H at -78°C (allows stopping at imine intermediate)

### 1.4 From Aromatic Compounds (Name Reactions)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Reaction Name | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ar-H (Benzene, toluene, anisole) | Aromatic formylation | CO + HCl | **Anhydrous AlCl₃/CuCl, 5-10 atm pressure, 0-50°C** | Ar-CHO (Aromatic aldehyde, e.g., Benzaldehyde) | **Gatterman-Koch reaction** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-CH₃ (Toluene, xylene) | Methyl oxidation (selective) | **CrO₂Cl₂** (Chromyl chloride) | CCl₄ or Et₂O, RT, gentle | Ar-CHO (Benzaldehyde from toluene) | **Etard reaction** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-CH₂-X (Benzyl halide) | Oxidation to acid then reduction | KMnO₄/acid or K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄ | Heat, aqueous acidic | Ar-COOH (intermediate) → Ar-CHO (DIBAL-H) | Multi-step | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Gatterman-Koch:** Only for electron-rich aromatics (e.g., benzene, anisole, not nitrobenzene)  
**Etard:** Very selective -CH₃ to -CHO oxidation without over-oxidation to acid

### 1.5 From Carboxylic Acid Derivatives

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ar-C≡N (Aromatic nitrile) | Selective hydrolysis to aldimine | **SnCl₂/HCl** (anhydrous) | RT or mild heat, then aqueous workup | Ar-CHO (Aromatic aldehyde) | **Stephen reaction**: Ar-C≡N → Ar-CH=NH·HCl → Ar-CHO | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COOH (Carboxylic acid) | Partial reduction | **DIBAL-H** | -78°C, ethereal, quick workup, stoichiometric | R-CHO (Aldehyde) + trace R-CH₂-OH | Difficult selectivity; over-reduction to alcohol common | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CO-O-Et (Ester) | Partial reduction | **DIBAL-H** | -78°C, ethereal | R-CHO (Aldehyde) | Selective at low temperature with stoichiometric control | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CO-Cl (Acid chloride) | Reduction to aldehyde | **H₂/Pd-BaSO₄** | RT-120°C | R-CHO | **Rosenmund reduction** (see 1.2) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Stephen Reaction:** Most reliable for aromatic aldehydes from nitriles

## SECTION 2: KETONE PREPARATION METHODS

### 2.1 From Secondary Alcohols (Oxidation - Most Common)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Selectivity | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ (2° Alcohol) | **K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄** | Acidic, RT to heat | R₁-CO-R₂ (Ketone) | **Complete oxidation to ketone; no over-oxidation** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ | **PCC** (Pyridinium chlorochromate) | CH₂Cl₂, RT | R₁-CO-R₂ | Selective; no over-oxidation | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ | **Jones Reagent** (CrO₃/H₂SO₄, acetone) | RT | R₁-CO-R₂ | Fast reaction | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ | **DMP** (Dess-Martin Periodinane) | CH₂Cl₂, RT | R₁-CO-R₂ | Mild, selective | 2024, 2023 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH(OH)-R₂ | **Swern Oxidation** | (COCl)₂/DMSO, -78°C, then Et₃N | R₁-CO-R₂ | **Inversion if stereogenic center** | 2024, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**All methods give ketone; no further oxidation (unlike primary alcohols)**  
**Best for JEE:** K₂Cr₂O₇ or PCC (both reliable)

### 2.2 From Aromatic Compounds - Friedel-Crafts Acylation

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ar-H (Benzene, toluene, anisole, etc.) | Aromatic acylation | **R-CO-Cl** (Acyl chloride) | **Anhydrous AlCl₃, RT to mild heat** | Ar-CO-R (Aromatic ketone) | **Friedel-Crafts acylation**; -OH and -NH₂ activate ring | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-H | Aromatic acylation (anhydride) | **(R-CO)₂O** (Acid anhydride) | AnhydrousAlCl₃, heat | Ar-CO-R | Less common; less reactive than acyl chloride | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-H | Via nitrile intermediate | **R-C≡N** (Nitrile) | Anhydrous AlCl₃, RT | Ar-CO-R (via complex) | Requires AlCl₃ as Lewis acid | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Limitations:** Only electron-rich aromatics (benzene, toluene, anisole)  
**Not possible with:** Nitrobenzene, benzonitrile, benzaldehyde (electron-poor)

### 2.3 From Alkenes (Ozonolysis)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent/Conditions | Product | Workup | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R₁-CH=CH-R₂ (Alkene, both carbons substituted) | Ozonolysis | **O₃** in inert solvent (CCl₄, CH₂Cl₂) | Primary ozonide (unstable intermediate) | **(CH₃)₂S** (Dimethyl sulfide, reductive) at -78°C or RT | R₁-CO-R₂ + R₂-CO-R₃ (Ketones) | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH=CH-R₂ | Ozonolysis | **O₃** | Primary ozonide | **H₂O₂** (Oxidative workup) | R₁-COOH + R₂-COOH (Carboxylic acids, not ketones) | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R₁-CH=CH-R₂ | Ozonolysis | **O₃** | Primary ozonide | **Zn/H₂O** (Reductive workup) | R₁-CHO + R₂-CHO (Aldehydes, not ketones) | 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Key:** Product depends on workup method  
**Selective:** Internal alkene (both C disubstituted) → Ketone cleanly with (CH₃)₂S workup

### 2.4 From Grignard + Nitrile

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Mechanism | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R'-C≡N (Nitrile) | Grignard addition to nitrile | **R-MgX** (Grignard reagent) | Dry ether, RT or mild heat | R-CO-R' (Ketone, after workup) | Grignard attacks nitrile C≡N → Imine → (H₃O⁺) → Ketone | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Advantage:** One step; clean ketone synthesis  
**Alternative to Friedel-Crafts:** When aromatic not available

### 2.5 From Alkynes (Hydration & Mercury)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-C≡C-R' (Internal alkyne) | Hydration (Markovnikov) | **H₂SO₄/HgSO₄** | Heat (333K), dilute acid | **Mixed ketones** (Markovnikov addition) | Both products possible if alkyne unsymmetrical | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-C≡C-H (Terminal alkyne) | Hydration (Markovnikov) | **H₂SO₄/HgSO₄** | Heat, dilute acid | **R-CO-CH₃** (Methyl ketone) | Specific product; Markovnikov adds to terminal C | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-C≡C-R' (Internal alkyne) | Hydration (anti-Markovnikov) | **BH₃ then H₂O₂/OH⁻** | Hydroboration-oxidation | Less hindered ketone predominates | Alternative to Markovnikov | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Markovnikov:** More substituted carbon gets OH (forms ketone more readily)  
**Terminal alkynes:** Specifically give methyl ketones

## SECTION 3: CARBOXYLIC ACID PREPARATION METHODS

### 3.1 From Primary Alcohols (Oxidation)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Mechanism | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-CH₂-OH (Primary alcohol) | **K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄** | Acidic, heat (excess reagent, azeotrope removal) | R-COOH (Carboxylic acid) | R-CH₂-OH → R-CHO (intermediate) → R-COOH | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **KMnO₄** | Acidic/neutral, heat | R-COOH | Oxidation to acid (MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CH₂-OH | **HNO₃** (Dilute) | Heat | R-COOH | Oxidative esterification pathway | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Mechanism:** Two-step oxidation (Alcohol → Aldehyde → Carboxylic acid)

### 3.2 From Aldehydes (Oxidation - Very Easy)

| Starting Material | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Ease | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-CHO (Aldehyde) | **K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄** | Acidic, mild to moderate heat | R-COOH | **Very easy; aldehydes readily oxidized** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CHO | **KMnO₄** | Cold acidic/neutral medium | R-COOH | Immediate oxidation (cold conditions work) | 2024, 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CHO | **Ag(NH₃)₂⁺** (Tollens reagent) | Alkaline, RT | R-COO⁻Ag⁺ + Ag (mirror) | Forms silver salt; silver mirror observation | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CHO | **HNO₃** (Dilute) | RT or mild heat | R-COOH | Oxidizing acid | 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Key:** Aldehydes are extremely easy to oxidize to carboxylic acids

### 3.3 From Aromatic Methyl Groups (Oxidation)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ar-CH₃ (Toluene, xylene, etc.) | Complete side chain oxidation | **KMnO₄** | **Acidic medium, heat** | Ar-COOH (Aromatic carboxylic acid) | **Entire -CH₃ group → COOH** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-CH₃ | Complete side chain oxidation | **K₂Cr₂O₇/H₂SO₄** | Acidic, heat | Ar-COOH | Alternative to KMnO₄ | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| Ar-CH₃ | Complete oxidation | **HNO₃** (Concentrated) | Heat (harsh conditions) | Ar-COOH | Vigorous oxidation; heating required | 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Complete oxidation:** Side chain fully oxidized to -COOH  
**Example:** Toluene → Benzoic acid; Xylene → Phthalic acid isomers

### 3.4 From Nitriles (Hydrolysis)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Mechanism | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-C≡N (Nitrile) | Acidic hydrolysis | **H⁺ (H₂SO₄ or HCl) + H₂O** | Heat (reflux, 100-150°C) | R-COOH (Carboxylic acid) | Protonated nitrile → Amide intermediate → Carboxylic acid | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-C≡N | Basic hydrolysis | **NaOH (aqueous) + H₂O** | Heat (reflux, 100-150°C) | R-COO⁻Na⁺ (Sodium carboxylate) | Nucleophilic attack on nitrile C; product is salt (acidify to get free acid) | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Mechanism:** R-C≡N → (H⁺) → R-C(=NH)-NH₂ (Imidate) → (H₂O) → R-COOH

### 3.5 From Grignard + CO₂ (One-Carbon Homologation)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-MgX (Grignard reagent) | CO₂ addition to Grignard | **CO₂** (dry, gaseous or dry ice) | Dry ether, RT | R-CO₂⁻MgX⁺ (Carboxylate salt complex) | Grignard attacks CO₂; forms carboxylate-magnesium complex | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-CO₂⁻MgX⁺ (Intermediate) | Aqueous acidic workup | **H⁺ (HCl or H₂SO₄) + H₂O** | RT | R-COOH (Carboxylic acid) | Simple protonation releases free acid | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Overall:** R-MgX + CO₂ → (workup) → R-COOH  
**Key:** Adds one carbon to chain; very reliable method

### 3.6 From Esters (Saponification/Hydrolysis)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product(s) | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-COO-R' (Ester) | Base-catalyzed hydrolysis | **NaOH (aqueous)** or KOH | Heat (reflux) | R-COO⁻Na⁺ (Sodium carboxylate) + R'-OH (Alcohol) | **Saponification**; irreversible | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COO⁻Na⁺ (Sodium salt) | Acidification | **H⁺ (HCl, H₂SO₄)** | RT or mild heat | R-COOH (Free carboxylic acid) | Simple acid-base; neutralizes salt | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COO-R' (Ester) | Acid-catalyzed hydrolysis | **H⁺ (H₂SO₄, HCl) + H₂O** | Heat (reflux) | R-COOH + R'-OH | Reversible (equilibrium); slow | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Saponification:** Common for fats/oils → Soap + Glycerol  
**Preferred method for acid production:** Base-catalyzed (saponification), then acidify

### 3.7 From Acid Chlorides (Hydrolysis)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-COCl (Acid chloride) | Hydrolysis | **H₂O** (Just water!) | Very mild; exothermic | R-COOH + HCl↑ | **Instant hydrolysis; very reactive** | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COCl | Hydrolysis (gentler) | **Dilute NaOH or KOH** | RT | R-COO⁻Na⁺ + NaCl | Forms salt; safer than H₂O | 2023, 2022 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |

**Key:** Acid chlorides are very reactive; instant hydrolysis with water

### 3.8 From Acid Anhydrides (Hydrolysis)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (R-CO)₂O (Acid anhydride) | Hydrolysis | **H₂O** | Mild heat | R-COOH + R-COOH (or mixed) | Hydrolysis of both acyl groups | 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| (R-CO)₂O | Hydrolysis | **NaOH (aqueous)** | RT or mild heat | R-COO⁻Na⁺ + R-COO⁻Na⁺ | Forms carboxylate salts | 2022, 2021 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Less reactive than acid chlorides; slower hydrolysis**

### 3.9 From Carboxylate Salts (Acidification - Simple)

| Starting Material | Method | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-COO⁻Na⁺ (Sodium carboxylate) | Acidification | **H⁺** (HCl, H₂SO₄, dilute) | RT or mild heat | R-COOH (Free carboxylic acid) | Simple acid-base neutralization | 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021 | ⭐⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐⭐ Adv |
| R-COO⁻ Mg²⁺ or Ca²⁺ (Salt) | Acidification | **H⁺** | RT | R-COOH | Reverse of salt formation | 2023, 2022 | ⭐ Mains, ⭐⭐ Adv |

**Easy:** Just acidify salt to recover free acid
