## SECTION 1: AMINE REACTIONS

### 1.1 Nucleophilic Substitution Reactions

#### 1.1.1 Reaction with Acid Chlorides (Acylation)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-NH₂** (1° amine) | R'-COCl | Base (pyridine), RT | R-NH-CO-R' (Amide) | **Secondary amide (2°)** |
| R₂NH (2° amine) | R'-COCl | Base, RT | R₂N-CO-R' (Amide) | **Tertiary amide (3°)** |
| R₃N (3° amine) | R'-COCl | Base, RT | NO REACTION | Quaternary N (no lone pair) |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

#### 1.1.2 Reaction with Alkyl Halides (Alkylation)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-NH₂ (1°) | R'-X (Alkyl halide) | Alcoholic, heat | R-NHR' (2° amine) + R-NR'₂ (3° amine) | **Mixture of products** |
| NH₃ (excess) | R-X | Alcoholic, heat | R-NH₂ (1° amine, main product) | Excess NH₃ favors 1° |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 1.2 Carbylamine Reaction (Isocyanide Formation - Test)

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Observation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-NH₂** (1° amine) | **CHCl₃ + KOH** | Alcoholic, heat | **R-N≡C** (Isocyanide) | **Foul-smelling (mustard oil smell)** |
| R₂NH (2° amine) | CHCl₃ + KOH | Alcoholic, heat | NO REACTION | Negative test |
| R₃N (3° amine) | CHCl₃ + KOH | Alcoholic, heat | NO REACTION | Negative test |

**Test:** Only primary amines give positive carbylamine test  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 1.3 Reaction with Nitrous Acid (HNO₂)

#### 1.3.1 Primary Aliphatic Amines

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-NH₂** (Aliphatic, 1°) | **NaNO₂ + HCl** | **Cold (0-5°C)** | **R-OH + N₂↑ + H₂O** | **Diazo → Carbocation → Alcohol + Alkene** |
| R-CH₂-NH₂ (1°, primary) | NaNO₂/HCl | Cold then warm | R-CH₂-OH (main) + R-CH=CH₂ (minor) | **E1 & SN1 mix** |

**Mechanism:** Diazonium intermediate unstable → Carbocation → Products

#### 1.3.2 Secondary Aliphatic Amines

| Reactant | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R₂NH** (2° aliphatic) | **NaNO₂ + HCl** | Cold (0-5°C) | **R₂N-N=O** (Nitroso amine) | **Yellow oily liquid; insoluble in water** |

**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 1.4 Hinsberg Reaction (Amine Classification Test)

| Amine Type | Reagent | Conditions | Product | Solubility in NaOH |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R-NH₂** (1° amine) | **C₆H₅-SO₂Cl** (Benzenesulfonyl chloride) | Pyridine or NaOH, RT | R-NH-SO₂-C₆H₅ (Sulfonamide) | **SOLUBLE** (acidic -NH) |
| R₂NH (2° amine) | C₆H₅SO₂Cl | Pyridine, RT | R₂N-SO₂-C₆H₅ (Sulfonamide) | **INSOLUBLE** (no -NH) |
| R₃N (3° amine) | C₆H₅SO₂Cl | Pyridine, RT | NO REACTION (solution remains clear) | — |

**Test:** Distinguishes primary, secondary, and tertiary amines  
**Year Tags:** 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021  
**JEE Level:** ⭐⭐⭐ Mains | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Mains+Advanced

### 5.1 Hofmann Bromamide Degradation (Loss of CO, Amine Synthesis)

| Reactant | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 | Product | Year Tags | JEE Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
