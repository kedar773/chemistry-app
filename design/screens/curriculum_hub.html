Aldol Condensation & Cross-Aldol

Aldehydes/ketones having at least one α-hydrogen undergo self-condensation in presence of dilute base (NaOH/Ba(OH)₂) to form β-hydroxyaldehydes.

Reaction Scheme Reagents: Dilute NaOH & Δ
2 CH₃-CHO ──(dil. NaOH)──> CH₃-CH(OH)-CH₂-CHO ──(Δ, -H₂O)──> CH₃-CH=CH-CHO
Product: But-2-en-1-al (Crotonaldehyde)
