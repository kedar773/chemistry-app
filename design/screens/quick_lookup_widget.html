Cannizzaro Reaction

Aldehydes & Ketones • Organic Chem

Disproportionation redox reaction of aldehydes lacking an α-hydrogen in the presence of concentrated base (50% NaOH or KOH).

Stoichiometric Equation
2 HCHO + conc. NaOH ⟶ CH₃OH + HCOONa
Formaldehyde ⟶ Methanol + Sodium Formate
lightbulb High-Yield Exam Points

Benzylic aldehydes (like Benzaldehyde) also undergo Cannizzaro. When cross-coupling with HCHO (Cross-Cannizzaro), formaldehyde is always oxidised to formic acid salt.
