1. Raoult's Law for Volatile Solutes

science Standard Statement

"For a solution of volatile liquids, the partial vapour pressure of each component in the solution is directly proportional to its mole fraction present in that solution."

Governing Formulation Binary Mix
p₁ = x₁ · p₁°
Component 1
p₂ = x₂ · p₂°
Component 2
Dalton's Total Pressure Law Expression
ptotal = p₁ + p₂ = (p₁° - p₂°)x₁ + p₂°
info Where p₁° and p₂° denote vapour pressures of pure components 1 & 2 respectively at given temperature T.

Exam Pitfall

For electrolytes undergoing dissociation (e.g., NaCl, BaCl₂) or association (e.g., Ethanoic Acid in Benzene), multiply colligative formulas by i:

ΔTb = i · Kb · m | π = i · CRT
Dissociation: i = 1 + (n - 1)α > 1 Association: i = 1 + (1/n - 1)α < 1
