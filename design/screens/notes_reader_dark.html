1. Raoult's Law for Volatile Solutes

verified NCERT Formal Principle

"For a solution of volatile liquids, the partial vapour pressure of each component in the solution is directly proportional to its mole fraction present in that solution."

Governing Formulation Binary Liquid Solution
Solvent (Comp 1) p₁ = x₁ · p₁°
Solute (Comp 2) p₂ = x₂ · p₂°
Dalton's Total Pressure Law P_total
Ptotal = p₁ + p₂ = (p₁° - p₂°)x₁ + p₂°
Where p₁°, p₂° are vapor pressures of pure components
